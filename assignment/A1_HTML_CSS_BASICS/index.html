Nuwakot District

Nuwakot District is one of the most beautiful districts of Nepal, known for its rich history, scenic hills, and cultural heritage. Located northwest of Kathmandu, the district offers breathtaking views of green valleys, terraced farmlands, and distant Himalayan peaks. It is home to the historic Nuwakot Durbar, a seven-story palace that reflects Nepal's medieval architecture and royal history. The district is also blessed with peaceful rivers, lush forests, and welcoming villages that preserve traditional Nepali customs and lifestyles. Its natural beauty, historical landmarks, and tranquil environment make Nuwakot a popular destination for visitors seeking both cultural exploration and scenic relaxation.



The Living History (Nuwakot Durbar)

Nuwakot is home to the historic Seven-Story Nuwakot Durbar (Palace), a massive fortress that reflects Nepal’s medieval Malla and Shah-era architecture. It served as a crucial strategic point during the unification of Nepal by King Prithvi Narayan Shah. Walking through the palace complex feels like stepping back in time, surrounded by intricate wood carvings, ancient temples, and old brick brickwork that echo stories of royal history. 2. Breathtaking, Uncrowded Landscapes Perched on a ridge northwest of Kathmandu, Nuwakot offers spectacular, panoramic views of deep green valleys, terraced farmlands, and the peaceful Trishuli and Tadi rivers flowing below. On a clear day, you get magnificent views of distant Himalayan peaks like Langtang and Ganesh Himal without the crowded commercial rush of other hill stations.
